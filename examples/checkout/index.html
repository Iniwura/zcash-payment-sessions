03 / THE MECHANISMDETERMINISTIC STATE

From intent
to certainty.

A precise state machine around every shielded payment. Nothing moves to paid until the evidence does.

01 REQUESTED Awaiting
payment↗

A ZIP-321 request binds the exact amount, recipient, memo and expiry to a durable session.

02 OBSERVED Detected↗

A wallet output is matched using amount, private memo and its unique chain identity.

03 SETTLING Confirming↗

Confirmations accumulate. Chain reorganizations remain explicit, never silently ignored.

04 VERIFIED Paid✓

Once the threshold is met, that exact payment is consumed and cannot credit another session.
