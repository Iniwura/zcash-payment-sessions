Bring a Zcash testnet wallet that can export its Unified Full Viewing Key (UFVK). ZPS only reads payment evidence. It cannot spend funds or create a wallet for you.

Export the viewing key from your existing testnet wallet. Never enter a seed phrase or private spending key. A UFVK is read-only but reveals wallet activity.

Where do I get a viewing key?

In a compatible testnet wallet, look for a viewing-key export. Copy its Unified Full Viewing Key, not its wallet address, recovery phrase or spending key. Some wallets cannot export a UFVK; you must use one that supports it.

A receiving address alone cannot be converted into a viewing key. ZPS does not generate or store spending keys.

Use the starting block height supplied by your wallet. This tells ZPS where to begin scanning. Entering 0 can trigger a very long historical scan; a height that's too recent can miss older payments.

Optional. Encrypts your viewing key locally in this browser so you can unlock it next time. Without it, you will need the UFVK again. A return visit may still require scanning.
